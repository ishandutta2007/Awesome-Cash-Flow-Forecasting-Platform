# Top Cash Flow Forecasting Platform Ecosystem


**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Cash Flow Forecasting, Liquidity Planning, Treasury Visibility, Scenario Modeling & Working Capital Intelligence*  
**Last updated: August 2026**


This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Cash Flow Forecasting**. These tools help finance and treasury teams consolidate bank, accounting, AR, AP, payroll, operating, and financing data to forecast future cash positions, model scenarios, identify liquidity gaps, monitor runway, and improve working-capital decisions.


**Examples** include CashFlowTool, Fluidly, CashAnalytics, Tesorio, HighRadius, Centime, Agicap, Futrli, and Cashforce. Modern platforms increasingly combine automated bank/ERP connectivity, AI-assisted forecasting, rolling forecasts, scenario analysis, cash visibility, and liquidity planning. For example, Centime provides rolling 13-week forecasts with configurable prediction rules, while Agicap combines cash-flow forecasting with banking, accounting, payments, and liquidity planning. ([centime.com](https://www.centime.com/products/cash-forecasting) :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}) ([agicap.com](https://agicap.com/en-us/) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1})


**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source cash-flow forecasting applications, forecasting engines, treasury dashboards, financial-planning projects, time-series forecasting libraries, ERP platforms, workflow automation, databases, BI tools, and infrastructure that can be combined to build self-hosted cash forecasting systems.


There are relatively few mature open-source products that directly replicate enterprise cash-flow forecasting suites. Therefore, this list intentionally includes both **complete/open-source forecasting projects** and **strong open-source building blocks** for bank-data ingestion, accounting integration, forecasting, scenario modeling, dashboards, alerts, and treasury analytics.


Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or repositories.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms


- **[CashFlowTool](https://www.cashflowtool.com/)**  
  Cash-flow forecasting and financial-planning platform focused on projecting cash balances, monitoring liquidity, and helping businesses understand future cash requirements.


- **[Fluidly](https://fluidly.com/)**  
  Cash-flow forecasting and financial-planning platform designed to connect accounting data with automated forecasts, scenarios, and business-planning workflows.


- **[CashAnalytics](https://www.cashanalytics.com/)**  
  Enterprise cash forecasting and treasury platform focused on automated cash forecasting, liquidity visibility, variance analysis, and treasury reporting.


- **[Tesorio](https://www.tesorio.com/)**  
  Cash-flow intelligence and working-capital platform combining accounts receivable, collections, payment behavior, and cash forecasting to improve liquidity visibility.


- **[HighRadius](https://www.highradius.com/)**  
  Enterprise finance and treasury platform with AI-powered cash-flow forecasting, AR/AP forecasting, bank connectivity, scenario analysis, and global liquidity visibility. HighRadius describes automated forecasts, scenario analysis, automated bank-statement sourcing, and AR forecasting as components of its cash-flow forecasting platform. ([highradius.com](https://www.highradius.com/product/cash-flow-forecasting-software/) :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2})


- **[Centime](https://www.centime.com/)**  
  Cash-management and forecasting platform combining banking, AP, AR, and cash forecasting. It provides rolling 13-week forecasting, configurable prediction rules, cash-plan scenarios, liquidity KPIs, and ERP connectivity. ([centime.com](https://www.centime.com/products/cash-forecasting) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3})


- **[Agicap](https://agicap.com/)**  
  Cash-management and treasury platform combining banking connectivity, cash-flow forecasting, liquidity planning, payments, transfers, collections, and bank reconciliation. Its forecasting functionality supports both short- and long-term forecasting and scenario planning. ([agicap.com](https://agicap.com/en-us/features/cashflow-forecast/) :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4})


- **[Futrli](https://www.futrli.com/)**  
  Financial forecasting and reporting platform that uses accounting data to produce forecasts across the P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow, including longer-term forecasts and scenario planning. ([futrli.com](https://www.futrli.com/) :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5})


- **[Cashforce](https://www.cashforce.com/)**  
  Cash-flow forecasting and treasury-management platform focused on working-capital intelligence, liquidity forecasting, cash visibility, and scenario analysis.


- **[Joiin](https://www.joiin.co/)**  
  Financial reporting and data-consolidation platform that can be used to combine accounting data into cash-flow and management reporting workflows.


- **[Fathom](https://www.fathomhq.com/)**  
  Financial-analysis and management-reporting platform supporting cash-flow analysis, forecasting, budgeting, and financial KPI reporting.


- **[Float](https://www.floatapp.com/)**
